Welcome to the MPDO Malilipot Planning Portal

This portal serves as an information and service platform of the Municipal Planning and Development Office of Malilipot, Albay. It provides access to municipal development plans, planning services, zoning information, civil society participation, and local development projects. Through transparent information, responsive services, and collaborative planning, we aim to help build a progressive, inclusive, and sustainable Municipality of Malilipot.
